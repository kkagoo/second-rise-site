How it works

Simple. Every day.

1

Do your morning check-in

Tap where you're sore, rate your energy, and flag any symptoms. Takes under a minute — and it actually changes what you're recommended today.

2

Get your recommendation

Second Rise combines your check-in with overnight recovery data from your wearable to suggest the right workout — strength, yoga, walk, or rest. No more decision fatigue.

3

Follow the guided session

Watch the video workout matched to your body today. When you're done, rate how it went — your feedback makes the next rec smarter.

4

Review your day

See how your actual strain compared to the plan — based on real steps and activity from your wearable. Then get a head start on tomorrow.
