Most fitness apps weren't built for this chapter.

They assume your body works the same as it did at 30. They don't account for disrupted sleep, joint sensitivity, hormonal shifts, or the fact that pushing harder sometimes makes things worse — not better.

Second Rise was.

🌡️

Menopause-aware

Knows your HRT status, pelvic floor history, and bone health. Recommends accordingly — not generically.

📉

Recovery-first

Uses your wearable data and check-in to dial intensity up or down. Rest is a recommendation, not a failure.

🔄

Adapts every day

No rigid programs. What your body needs on Monday is different from Friday — Second Rise adjusts with you.

Your day with Second Rise

Under a minute in the morning.
The right workout by the time you finish coffee.

01

Check in

Rate your energy, tap where you're sore, note any symptoms. Takes under 60 seconds.

02

Get your recommendation

Claude AI combines your check-in with overnight wearable data and picks the right session for today — strength, yoga, mobility, or rest.

03

Follow the session

Guided video matched to your body today. When you're done, rate how it felt so the next rec gets smarter.

04

See the pattern

Track how your actual strain compares to the plan over time. Understand what your body is telling you.
