Wearable Integrations

Second Rise connects to consumer fitness wearables and wellness platforms — including Oura Ring, WHOOP, Fitbit, Apple Health, Google Health, Withings, and Garmin — for the sole purpose of personalizing fitness recommendations. These are consumer-grade wellness devices and platforms, not FDA-regulated medical devices.

The App reads only general wellness metrics: sleep duration and stages, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), and step count. Second Rise does not access, read, or use blood pressure readings, ECG data, blood glucose measurements, or any other regulated medical measurements from any connected device or platform. Specifically, while Withings manufactures some FDA-cleared medical devices, Second Rise only reads sleep and heart rate data from the Withings wellness API.

By connecting a device or platform, you authorize Second Rise to access and store the wellness metrics described above. You can disconnect any integration at any time from your Profile page.
