Second Rise Beta Testing

Thank you for helping test Second Rise! This guide tells you everything you need to do.

⏱ One small ask: To count as an official tester, the app needs to see daily activity for 14 days in a row — if there's a gap, the counter resets. We know life gets busy! The morning check-in takes under 60 seconds so a quick daily reminder on your phone goes a long way. Thank you so much for making this possible 💙

2 Create your account

Open Second Rise and tap Sign up to create a free account with your email and a password. No credit card needed.

Already have an account from testing before? Just log in with your existing credentials.

3 Set up your profile

Tap the Profile tab (bottom right) and fill in what feels comfortable:

  • Age range
  • Where you are in your menopause journey
  • HRT status (optional)
  • Any joint issues or equipment you have access to

If you have a wearable (Oura, WHOOP, Fitbit, Apple Health, or Google Fit/Pixel Watch), connect it here for personalized recommendations.

4 What to test each day

Try each of these at least once over 14 days

Connect your wearable (do this on day 1)

Profile tab → connect Oura, WHOOP, Google Fit, Fitbit, or Apple Health. Does the connection work? Do your stats (sleep, HRV, recovery) appear on the home screen the next morning?

Morning check-in (do this EVERY day)

Home screen → Start check-in. Rate your energy, tap any sore areas on the body map. Takes under 60 seconds and counts as your daily usage.

View your workout recommendation

After check-in, tap "View workout". Does the recommendation match how you feel? Is it appropriate for your fitness level and wearable data?

Start a workout session

Tap "Start Session" on a recommended workout. Watch part of the video. Does it play? Are the rest timers working?

Browse videos by type

Home screen → tap Strength, Yoga, Pilates, or Mobility. Do videos load and play correctly?

End-of-day review

In the evening, go to the home screen and tap "End-of-day review" (appears after you complete check-in). Does it show how your actual activity compared to the plan?

Weekly reflection (after 7 days)

Look for the weekly reflection prompt after day 7. Complete it and tell us if the questions feel relevant.
